Creating Strong Passwords

A long, unique password is your first line of defense against hackers.

What Makes a Strong Password

A strong password should be at least 12-16 characters long. It should include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Most importantly, avoid personal information like your birthday or pet's name.

The Power of Passphrases

Passphrases are several random words joined together, like "BlueCoffeeRocket!42". They are much harder for computers to crack but far easier for you to remember than a string of random characters.

Quick Security Tips

  • Use a unique passwords for every single account.
  • Change your password immediately if a site reports a breach.
  • Combine passphrases with symbols for maximum security.
