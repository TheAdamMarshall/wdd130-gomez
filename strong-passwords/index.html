What Makes A Strong Password?

A strong password should be long, unique, and difficult to guess. It should include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using names, birthdays, or common words.

Using Passphrases

A passphrase is a longer password made from several words. For example, a phrase like: BlueCoffeeRocket!42 is much stronger than a short password like "abc123". Passphrases are easier to remember but harder for attackers to guess.

Password Tips

  • Use at least 12–16 characters
  • Avoid common words or personal information
  • Do not reuse passwords on multiple websites
